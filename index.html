002
Active · Final Year

Linguix-NG — Intelligent Normalisation Middleware

A final-year research project tackling one of West Africa's most pressing digital-inclusion challenges: the linguistic friction between how Nigerians naturally speak and the formal English that enterprise platforms expect. Linguix-NG is an NLP middleware that translates Nigerian Pidgin and native-language input into standard English in real time, enabling seamless integration with FinTech and HealthTech APIs that would otherwise reject informal text as malformed data.

  • Curated a specialised parallel corpus of Nigerian dialects (Pidgin, Yoruba-influenced English) paired with formal English equivalents
  • Hybrid architecture combining rule-based lexical mapping with Transformer-based Neural Machine Translation for robust normalisation
  • Designed for low-latency middleware deployment sits between user input and downstream enterprise APIs with zero changes to existing backends
  • Targets real-world FinTech and HealthTech platforms where linguistic mismatches lock out 75M+ Nigerian Pidgin speakers
  • Advances African NLP research with a reusable open corpus and benchmarks for Nigerian language normalisation tasks
