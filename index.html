Linguix-NG : Pidgin and English Translation
Final Year Project

Nigerian Pidgin is spoken daily by millions, but banking and healthcare platforms are written for formal English. Linguix-NG translates both ways between Pidgin and English across Healthcare, Banking, Retail and Customer Service. The fine tuned model reached a BLEU score of 74.76.

  • Built a domain specific Pidgin and English parallel corpus of about 8,000 sentence pairs, which did not exist before.
  • Fine tuned a MarianMT model. A Transformer trained from scratch scored about 0.33 BLEU, so pre-trained initialisation made the difference.
  • Routes low quality output to Groq LLaMA 3.3 as a fallback, served through a Flask API with translation history logging and a React frontend.

SmartStock : AI Digital Credit Book
Nomba Hackathon 2026

Replaces paper credit ledgers for Nigerian shop owners. Four backend services share one PostgreSQL database: auth, a credit engine, AI scoring and payment webhooks.

  • Every customer gets a Nomba virtual account, and webhooks are signature verified with idempotent payment recording.
  • A Random Forest model scores default risk from 0 to 100, trained on 2,868 synthetic profiles (ROC-AUC 0.68).
  • Built solo. Fixed a real production issue: Railway blocks outbound SMTP, so reminder emails moved to the Resend API.
