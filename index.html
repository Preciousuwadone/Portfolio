Linguix-NG : Intelligent Normalization Middleware
Active · Final Year

75M+ Nigerians speak Pidgin daily, but enterprise banking and healthcare platforms are built for formal English. Linguix-NG sits between user and API, translating Pidgin into standard English in real time, no backend changes required. Currently hitting a 74.76 BLEU score against a target of 50.

  • Curated a Pidgin-to-formal-English parallel corpus that didn't previously exist.
  • Hybrid architecture: fast rule-based mapping plus a Transformer model for nuanced cases.
